# **App Name**: Alma Lírica

## Core Features:

- Poem Display: Render the primary poem content in an elegant, centered format with interactive hover effects.
- AI Verse Generator: A generative AI tool that crafts new, short verses or comforting affirmations based on a user's input of mood or keywords, reflecting the introspective and supportive tone of the original poem.
- Verse Management: Allow users to save their favorite generated verses or specific lines from the main poem for later reflection.
- Dynamic Penguin Mascot: Integrate and maintain the charming 8-bit pixel art penguin, continuously animating across the bottom of the content area.
- Subtle Background Ambiance: Preserve the animated particle background, creating a serene and immersive atmosphere for reading.

## Style Guidelines:

- The visual theme is dark and contemplative, drawing inspiration from a serene night sky. Primary action/highlight color: A muted periwinkle-blue (#A2B7DB), providing a gentle contrast against the dark background. Background color: A very dark, desaturated blue (#171B20), forming a deep and calming canvas. Accent color: A deep indigo-purple (#402C82), offering a richer, slightly more vibrant touch for secondary elements.
- Body and headline font: 'Literata' (serif), chosen for its literary, vintage, and slightly formal feel that complements the poetic content.
- Use minimalist, line-art style icons that convey concepts of reflection, support, and gentle whimsy, consistent with the overall aesthetic and the 8-bit penguin's charm. Avoid overly bold or cartoonish designs.
- Employ a clean, centered layout for textual content, ensuring readability and focus on the poem or generated verses. Utilize subtle visual separators or cards for different content blocks, such as AI input and output, while maintaining spaciousness.
- Retain the continuous, subtle particle animation in the background to maintain an ethereal ambiance. Preserve the existing 8-bit penguin's smooth horizontal movement as a unique, playful element.
